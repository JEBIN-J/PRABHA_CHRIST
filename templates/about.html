The Artist

Where emotion meets timelessness

Prabha Chris is a visionary artist dedicated to exploring the profound intersection of human emotion and digital form. Her work is characterized by its ability to evoke deep introspection and a sense of timelessness, transcending traditional boundaries of media.

With a practice that spans intricate oil paintings, expressive sketches, and cutting-edge digital art, Prabha's creations are a testament to her belief that art is a deliberate choice made to reveal what is often unseen.
